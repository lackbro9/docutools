    How to use this tool

    1. Upload a PNG image.
    2. Choose a JPG quality level.
    3. Click "Convert to JPG".
    4. Preview and download the JPG file.

    Limitations

    JPG does not support transparency. Any transparent areas in your PNG are filled with a white background during conversion.

    Privacy

    This tool processes your file locally in your browser. Your file is not uploaded to a server as part of this operation.

    Frequently asked questions

    What happens to transparent parts of my PNG?

    They are filled with solid white, since the JPG format has no transparency channel.

    Is my image uploaded anywhere?

    No, conversion happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API.
