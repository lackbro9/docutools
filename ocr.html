OCR - Image to Text

Extract text from a photo or scanned image using on-device optical character recognition.

Supports JPG, PNG and WebP, up to 15 MB. English text works best.

    Recognized text

    How to use this tool

    1. Upload a photo or scanned image containing text.
    2. Click "Extract Text (OCR)" and wait while the recognition engine processes the image.
    3. Review the recognized text, then download it as a .txt file.

    Limitations

    OCR accuracy depends heavily on image quality, resolution, lighting, and how clean and horizontal the text is. Handwriting, low-resolution photos, and unusual fonts are recognized less reliably. Recognition runs entirely in your browser and can take anywhere from a few seconds to over a minute depending on image size and your device, especially the first time (when the recognition model is downloaded).

    Privacy

    This tool processes your image locally in your browser using the Tesseract.js library. The image itself is not sent to any external OCR API; the only network request involved is downloading the OCR engine and its language data from a public CDN the first time you use this tool.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why is OCR slow the first time I use it?

    The recognition engine and its English language data need to be downloaded from a CDN the first time you use this tool on a given device; afterwards, your browser can reuse the cached copy.

    Does it support languages other than English?

    This build is configured for English text recognition. Other languages could be added in a future version.

    Is my image sent to an external OCR service?

    No. Recognition happens on your device using Tesseract.js; your image is never uploaded to an OCR API.
