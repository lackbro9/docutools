    How to use this tool

    1. Upload a JPG or JPEG image.
    2. Click "Convert to PNG".
    3. Preview and download the PNG file.

    Limitations

    PNG is a lossless format, so converting a JPG to PNG usually produces a larger file; it does not add detail that was not already in the original JPG.

    Privacy

    This tool processes your file locally in your browser. Your file is not uploaded to a server as part of this operation.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will converting to PNG improve image quality?

    No. It preserves whatever quality the original JPG already had; PNG cannot recover detail lost during the original JPG compression.

    Is my image uploaded anywhere?

    No, conversion happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API.
