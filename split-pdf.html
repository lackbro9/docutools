    How to use this tool

    1. Upload a PDF file. DocuTools will show you how many pages it has.
    2. Type the page numbers or ranges you want to keep, such as 1-3,5,8-10.
    3. Click "Split PDF".
    4. Download the new, smaller PDF.

    Limitations

    Password-protected PDFs cannot be split until they are unlocked. Very large PDFs may take longer to process because everything happens in your browser.

    Privacy

    This tool processes your file locally in your browser. Your file is not uploaded to a server as part of this operation.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does this remove the pages from my original file?

    No. Your original file is untouched; DocuTools creates a brand new PDF containing only the pages you chose.

    Can I select pages out of order?

    Selected pages are always kept in their original order in the output PDF. Use the Page Extractor tool if you need more visual control over selection.

    Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

    No, splitting happens locally in your browser using the pdf-lib library.
