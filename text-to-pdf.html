Text to PDF

Turn plain text into a formatted, paginated PDF with sensible margins.

Local processing

Your PDF is ready

How to use this tool

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Choose a page size and font size.
  3. Click "Convert to PDF". Long text automatically wraps and flows across multiple pages.
  4. Download the finished PDF.

Limitations

This tool supports plain text only: no bold, italics, images, or custom fonts. For richly formatted documents, consider a full word processor and export to PDF from there.

Privacy

This tool processes your file locally in your browser. Your file is not uploaded to a server as part of this operation.

Frequently asked questions

Does it support very long text?

Yes. Text automatically flows onto as many pages as needed.

Can I control the margins?

Margins are fixed to a comfortable, readable default for both supported page sizes.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No, the PDF is generated locally in your browser using the pdf-lib library.
