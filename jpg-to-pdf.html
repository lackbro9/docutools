    How to use this tool

    1. Click the drop zone or drag JPG files onto it to add them.
    2. Reorder images using the up and down arrows, or remove any you do not want.
    3. Click "Convert to PDF" to build the file.
    4. Click "Download PDF" to save the result to your device.

    Limitations

    Very large images or a large number of files can be slow to process on low-powered devices because everything happens in your browser's memory. Extremely large images are automatically scaled down to keep the PDF a reasonable size.

    Privacy

    This tool processes your file locally in your browser. Your file is not uploaded to a server as part of this operation.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will this reduce my image quality?

    Images larger than roughly 1600 by 2000 pixels are scaled down to keep the PDF a manageable size; smaller images are placed at their original resolution.

    Can I add PNG files too?

    This tool only accepts JPG/JPEG files. Use the Images to PDF tool if you need to mix JPG and PNG files.

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    No. The PDF is built locally in your browser using the pdf-lib library.
