    How to use this tool

    1. Upload an image.
    2. Choose JPEG or WebP as the output format, and pick a quality level.
    3. Click "Compress Image".
    4. Check the measured size comparison, then download the result.

    Limitations

    Compression always re-encodes the image as JPEG or WebP, both of which are lossy formats; transparency in PNG images is flattened onto a white background when JPEG is chosen. Some images, especially ones that are already small or simple graphics, may not shrink further.

    Privacy

    This tool processes your file locally in your browser. Your file is not uploaded to a server as part of this operation.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why choose WebP over JPEG?

    WebP often produces a smaller file at similar visual quality, but it is disabled here if your specific browser cannot encode it.

    What happens to transparent PNGs?

    If you compress to JPEG, transparent areas are filled with white, since JPEG has no transparency support. Choose WebP to keep transparency.

    Is my image uploaded anywhere?

    No, compression happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API.
