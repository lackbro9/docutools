    How to use this tool

    1. Add two or more PDF files using the drop zone.
    2. Use the arrows to put the files in the order you want them to appear.
    3. Click "Merge PDFs".
    4. Download the combined PDF.

    Limitations

    Password-protected PDFs cannot be merged until they are unlocked, and very large files may take longer to process because merging happens in your browser's memory.

    Privacy

    This tool processes your file locally in your browser. Your file is not uploaded to a server as part of this operation.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    Up to 20 files in a single batch, each up to 60 MB.

    Will bookmarks or forms be preserved?

    Page content and page order are preserved. Complex interactive form fields or bookmarks may not carry over exactly.

    Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

    No, merging happens locally in your browser using the pdf-lib library.
